API key

1. Make my Torn key or: make a Limited Access key

Opens Torn with every permission the site uses (stats, attacks, companies, net worth). Then tap Check my key below to see that all of it works. Tap Create, copy the key. Made your key earlier? Make a new one, or edit it in Torn's API settings and tick Company and Torn.

    FF Scouter (stat estimates)

    FF Scouter needs a free one-time sign up with your Torn key.

    Jackpot alerts

    Shows a message at the top of the Mug Finder when a seller has an item worth the amount below or more, and all the rules below match. The auto hunt feed on the Mug Finder uses the same stats, hospital and offline rules (without the item worth amount).

    Inactive earner wages

    Torn does not show wages, so the Inactive earners tab guesses: wage per day x days inactive. Unless you set a wage for a company type, the wage is a share of that company's real daily income split between its employees (Torn caps pay at $25m a day).

    Fixed wage per company type (at 10 stars)
    Save your Torn key first to load the company types.

    Mugging bonuses

    Used for every predicted mug and expected profit on the finders. A mug takes about 5% of the target's cash. Masterful Looting levels and the Plunder bonus on your weapon raise that. Example: level 10 (+50%) with 20% Plunder takes 5% x (1 + 0.5 + 0.2) = 8.5%.

    Your email

    Used for account recovery and security notices, including when your email changes. Changing it needs your current password.

    Invite someone

    Make a single-use link that lasts 7 days. You can have one open at a time. Tap X to close it and make a new one. Send it to the person. They apply with it, then type your username to get in.
