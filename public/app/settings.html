API key

One key does everything. It is saved in this browser only. Each request passes through our server and is never stored there.

Make my Torn key

Opens Torn with the right permissions filled in (including attacks, which the leaderboard uses to read your mugs). Tap Create there, copy the key, and paste it below.

Stat estimates come from FF Scouter, which uses this same key once you have registered at ffscouter.com (one time, free).

Advanced: separate FF Scouter key

The key is stored encrypted on our server. Leave this off to keep it in this browser only.

Jackpot alerts

Shows a message at the top of the Mug Finder when a seller has an item worth the amount below or more, and all the rules below match. The auto hunt feed on the Mug Finder uses the same stats, hospital and offline rules (without the item worth amount).

Your email

Used for account recovery and security notices, including when your email changes. Changing it needs your current password.

Invite someone

Make a single-use link that lasts 7 days. You can have one open at a time. Tap X to close it and make a new one. Send it to the person. They apply with it, then type your username to get in.
